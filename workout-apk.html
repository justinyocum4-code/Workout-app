Train smart before you train hard

  • Warm up for 3 to 5 minutes with easy marching, arm circles, and unloaded versions of your first movement.
  • Choose a resistance that leaves about two good repetitions in reserve. Form should stay steady from first rep to last.
  • Stop if you feel sharp, pinching, or sudden pain. Muscle effort is expected; joint pain is not.
  • For ab roller work, shorten the rollout as soon as your lower back starts to arch.
Move at your own pace. Consistency beats a perfect workout.
